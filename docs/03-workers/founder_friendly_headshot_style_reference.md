# Founder-Friendly Headshot Style Reference

Style target: modern startup / Silicon Valley / founder-friendly.

## Visual direction
- photorealistic business portrait
- relaxed confidence
- modern startup founder aesthetic
- soft natural light
- clean neutral background with a subtle warm gradient
- smart casual to polished business attire
- approachable, intelligent, energetic
- shot at eye level
- shallow depth of field
- high-end LinkedIn / startup company profile look
- no props, no text, no watermark

## Sample prompt
Photorealistic founder-friendly Silicon Valley style business headshot of a KOHD executive, modern startup aesthetic, relaxed confident expression, clean neutral studio background with subtle warm gradient, soft natural light, eye-level framing, shallow depth of field, crisp focus on eyes, smart casual / polished startup attire, authentic skin texture, premium LinkedIn profile photo, approachable and intelligent, no text, no watermark, no props.

## Negative prompt
- cartoon
- illustration
- overly formal corporate studio pose
- harsh shadows
- busy background
- exaggerated smile
- fake plastic skin
- low resolution
- text
- watermark
- props

## Notes
This is the style baseline for all KOHD worker and advisory board portraits.
