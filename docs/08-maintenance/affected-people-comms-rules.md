# Affected People Comms Rules

Source of truth: RELGOV-10

## Who gets notified
- Users explicitly named by the owner
- Service owners for impacted systems
- CXO/advisory stakeholders when the change affects governance or access
- Any person whose service/workflow may be interrupted

## When to notify
- Before the change window
- Again if the window slips materially
- After completion with outcome / rollback / validation summary

## Message content
- What is changing
- When it is changing
- What systems may be affected
- What to watch for
- Who to contact if there is a problem
- Link back to Linear

## Guardrails
- Keep messages short and actionable
- Do not include secrets or internal-only sensitive details
- Prefer a single source of recipient truth, not manual ad hoc lists
